# Skill 03 — Futuristic Virtual Office Design

## Mission
Design a cohesive miniature technology-company office with readable departments and purposeful navigation.

## Art direction
- Futuristic, premium, clean, corporate sci-fi.
- Dark graphite/navy base; restrained cyan, blue, and violet accents.
- Glass walls and controlled emissive signage; avoid excessive bloom/neon.
- Isometric/orthographic composition with clear floor plan.
- Use consistent scale for desks, chairs, monitors, doors, avatars, and room labels.

## Initial floor plan
1. Reception / Supervisor.
2. Research / Haiku.
3. Analysis / Sonnet.
4. Strategy / Opus.
5. Trading Operations / Decision Engineer.
6. Market Monitoring / Market Monitor.
7. Archive / Data Archivist.

Rooms should be visually distinct but connected by corridors. The user must be able to understand the office layout at a glance.

## Scene design checklist
- Floor base and room boundaries.
- Walls/partitions with openings and glass sections.
- Door/corridor navigation.
- Lighting hierarchy and readable labels.
- Workstation per worker, meeting area, reception and archive storage.
- Selection highlight for worker/workstation.
- Avoid unnecessary particles and expensive effects.

## Acceptance criteria
- All seven rooms/workstations are visible from the default camera.
- Labels remain readable at normal zoom.
- The selected worker is visually identifiable.
- Office remains coherent at different window sizes.
